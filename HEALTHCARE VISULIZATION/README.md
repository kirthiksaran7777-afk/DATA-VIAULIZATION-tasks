# Healthcare Data Visualization, Cost Relationship & Policy Insights

##  Project Overview

This project focuses on visualizing healthcare data, analyzing the relationship between medical costs and patient characteristics, identifying admission trends, and generating useful healthcare management insights.

The project uses Python-based data visualization and statistical analysis techniques to understand billing patterns, insurance provider differences, patient admissions, and relationships between age, hospital stay duration, and medical costs.

---

##  Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Compare billing amounts across different medical conditions.
2. Compare billing amounts across insurance providers.
3. Build stacked bar charts for healthcare cost analysis.
4. Create violin plots to understand billing amount distributions.
5. Analyze patient admission trends over time.
6. Identify possible seasonal increases in hospital admissions.
7. Generate a correlation matrix for age, hospital stay duration, and medical cost.
8. Develop an executive summary with healthcare management recommendations.

---

##  Dataset

**Dataset Name:** `healthcare_dataset.csv`

The dataset contains healthcare-related information such as:

* Patient details
* Age
* Gender
* Medical condition
* Admission date
* Discharge date
* Admission type
* Insurance provider
* Billing amount
* Hospital stay duration
* Medical codes

---

##  Data Visualization

### 1. Stacked Bar Chart

Stacked bar charts are used to compare billing amounts across:

* Medical conditions
* Insurance providers
* Admission categories

The charts help identify differences in healthcare costs between different groups.

Example:

```text
Medical Condition
       ↓
Insurance Provider
       ↓
Billing Amount
```

---

### 2. Violin Plot

Violin plots are used to visualize the distribution of billing amounts.

They help understand:

* Median billing amount
* Distribution of costs
* Spread of billing values
* Possible outliers
* Differences between medical conditions

Example analysis:

```text
Medical Condition → Billing Amount Distribution
Insurance Provider
```
